About Urban Threads

Urban Threads is a clothing store based in Johannesburg, Gauteng. The store was founded in 2022 with the aim of providing trendy and affordable clothing.

We offer fashion for women, men and kids. Our focus is on creating a simple and enjoyable shopping experience while giving customers access to stylish clothing at affordable prices.
